A machine that
turns itself off

The useless machine does one thing: when you switch it on, an arm comes out and switches it off again. Underneath it there is a small game, because a page should do something.

The toy

Press the switch.

It will turn itself off. That is the whole machine — the same idea as the wooden box that has been sold since the 1960s.

Click the switch — or reach it with Tab and press Enter.

The game

Catch the switch.

Twenty seconds. A switch appears somewhere in the box, you catch it, and it moves. The best score stays for as long as the page is open.
