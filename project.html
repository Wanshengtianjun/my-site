I could build

Not a wish list. Each idea names a real reader and one page that would have to be good. The last one is the one I started, and it is already online.

The list

Five ideas, five readers.

Written quickly first, judged afterwards. A four-page site done well beats a twelve-page site half-built.

  1. 01

    Block by Block

    A beginner’s guide to Minecraft: Java Edition — the controls, the first night, and the small things nobody explains.

    For someone who has just bought Java Edition, or switched from Bedrock and does not want to memorise a wiki.

  2. 02

    Wenchang Eats

    Where the locals eat within walking distance of campus: a map, a short list per street, and what to order.

    For a first-year student who arrived two weeks ago and does not know the town yet.

  3. 03

    Redstone Cookbook

    Ten small redstone builds explained in plain language: what it does, what you need, and why the repeater is where it is.

    For a player who can follow a build video but freezes when the video ends.

  4. 04

    Surf Window

    A surf forecast for the Hainan coast written in words rather than numbers: “small and clean before ten, messy after lunch.”

    For a beginner who wants to know whether tomorrow is worth getting up for.

  5. 05

    25 Minutes

    A focus timer that looks like a sheet of paper: one button, a large number, and a line that fills as the time passes.

    For a classmate in the library who studies in short blocks and loses track of them.

The one I chose

Block by Block

I picked the first idea because I could name the reader exactly — a friend who bought Java Edition and spent his first evening asking me which key opens the inventory. The guide answers that, then walks through the first night in nine steps.
